So you want to start photography but your budget cuma RM 2,000? Good news — you don't need to break the bank to get a solid camera in Malaysia. Whether you're a student, a parent who wants to capture family moments, or someone thinking of jadi part-time photographer, there's a DSLR (or mirrorless) out there for you.

But here's the thing: buying camera in Malaysia can be tricky. Gray import vs authorized dealer, warranty issues, and Shopee sellers who describe a camera as "like new" when it's actually been through a war. This guide will help you navigate all that.

## Why RM 2,000 Is the Sweet Spot for Beginners

At this price point, you get:
- A capable APS-C sensor (way better than any phone)
- Interchangeable lenses (upgrade path!)
- Decent autofocus for stills
- Manual controls to actually learn photography
- Resale value when you upgrade later

**What you DON'T get:** 4K video, blazing-fast burst shooting, or professional low-light performance. But for learning? More than enough.

## Malaysian Context: Authorized vs Gray Import

Before we dive into the cameras, here's a quick reality check:

| Type | Price | Warranty | Risk |
|------|-------|----------|------|
| Authorized Malaysia | Higher (RM 1,800-2,000) | Canon/Nikon Malaysia warranty | Low |
| Gray Import | Lower (RM 1,200-1,600) | Shop warranty only | Medium-High |
| Used/Refurbished | Lowest (RM 800-1,400) | Depends on seller | High |

**Our advice:** If this is your first camera, go authorized. The peace of mind is worth the extra RM 300-400. Stores like YL Camera, Foto Shangri-La, and Shopee Mall official stores are your safest bets.

## Top 7 Cameras Under RM 2,000

### 1. Canon EOS 250D (Rebel SL3) — Best All-Rounder
**Price:** ~RM 1,899 (body only, authorized)

The world's smallest DSLR with a vari-angle touchscreen. Perfect for beginners who want to vlog too.

**Pros:**
- Dual Pixel AF in live view (amazing for video)
- 4K video (cropped, but hey, it's there)
- Guided UI for beginners
- Excellent battery life

**Cons:**
- 4K has a crop factor
- AF points limited to 9 in viewfinder

**Best for:** Students, vloggers, anyone who wants photo + video

---

### 2. Nikon D3500 — Best Battery Life
**Price:** ~RM 1,699 (with kit lens, authorized)

Discontinued but still widely available. The D3500 is a photography purist's dream at this price.

**Pros:**
- 1,550 shots per charge (insane!)
- Excellent image quality
- Compact for a DSLR
- Great grip feel

**Cons:**
- No touchscreen
- No microphone input
- No 4K video

**Best for:** Pure photographers, travel shooters, those who hate charging batteries

---

### 3. Canon EOS 2000D — Best Value New
**Price:** ~RM 1,599 (with kit lens, authorized)

The most affordable Canon DSLR you can buy new with full Malaysia warranty.

**Pros:**
- WiFi for easy phone transfer
- 24MP sensor
- Very affordable

**Cons:**
- No touchscreen
- Basic autofocus
- No 4K

**Best for:** Absolute beginners on tight budget

---

### 4. Sony A6100 — Best Mirrorless Alternative
**Price:** ~RM 2,199 (slightly over, but watch for sales)

Okay, this one slightly exceeds RM 2,000, but during Shopee 3.3 or 6.6 sales, it drops to RM 1,899.

**Pros:**
- 425 phase-detect AF points
- Real-time Eye AF
- 4K video
- Tiny body

**Cons:**
- Over budget at normal price
- Sony menu system (legendary for being confusing)
- Small battery

**Best for:** Those who want mirrorless autofocus magic

---

### 5. Canon 60D — Best Used/Refurbished Deal
**Price:** ~RM 900-1,200 (used, body only)

The legendary workhorse. Read our full [Canon 60D 2026 review](link) to see why this 2010 camera still has fans.

**Pros:**
- Articulating screen
- Solid build (magnesium alloy)
- Cheap lenses available
- Pro-level controls

**Cons:**
- No warranty (when buying used)
- No video AF
- Older sensor tech

**Best for:** Serious beginners who want pro controls cheap

---

### 6. Nikon D5600 — Best Touchscreen Experience
**Price:** ~RM 1,899 (with kit lens, authorized)

The D5600 is what happens when Nikon tries to make a camera for the Instagram generation.

**Pros:**
- Flip touchscreen
- SnapBridge (auto phone transfer)
- 39 AF points
- Time-lapse built-in

**Cons:**
- No 4K video
- SnapBridge can be finicky

**Best for:** Social media shooters, travelers

---

### 7. Fujifilm X-T200 — Best for Style & Color
**Price:** ~RM 2,099 (often discounted to RM 1,899)

The prettiest camera on this list, with film simulations that make JPEGs look amazing.

**Pros:**
- Gorgeous retro design
- Film simulations (Classic Chrome, Velvia, etc.)
- Vari-angle touchscreen
- Lightweight

**Cons:**
- Fujifilm lens ecosystem is pricier
- Autofocus not as fast as Sony/Canon

**Best for:** Style-conscious shooters, those who love Fujifilm colors
