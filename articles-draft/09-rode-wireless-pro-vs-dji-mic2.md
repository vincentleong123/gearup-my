## Real-World Tests

### Outdoor Test: KLCC Park, Hujan Rehat
Range test dengan speaker jalan 100 meter. Both maintain signal. Rode sedikit lebih stable bila ada obstacles (pokok, bangunan).

### Wedding Test: Dewan Gemilang, Shah Alam
Rode 32-bit float internal recording save the day bila receiver setting salah. Backup audio dalam transmitter means takde retake needed.

DJI Mic 2 lebih mudah setup — touchscreen intuitive. Tapi takde backup recording.

### Street Interview: Petaling Street
Both handle crowd noise well. DJI Mic 2 slightly better noise rejection dalam mode "Noise Cancellation." Rode more natural sounding.

### Indoor Studio: 4x4 meter room
Both excellent. No difference noticeable dalam edit.
