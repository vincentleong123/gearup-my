## Performance in Malaysian Conditions

### Humidity Tolerance
Both lights handle Malaysian humidity well. Tapi Godox fan system lebih aggressive — better for long shoots tanpa aircond. Aputure runs cooler overall tapi output lebih rendah.

### Heat Dissipation (No AC Studio Test)
Tested dalam bilik 3x3 meter, fan off, 30 minit continuous:

| Light | Body Temp | Room Temp Rise |
|-------|-----------|----------------|
| Godox SL150III | 62°C | +3°C |
| Aputure 100x | 48°C | +2°C |

Godox panas sikit tapi tak ada masalah. Just jangan sentuh body selepas 30 minit.

## Use Case Matrix

### Product Photography
**Winner: Tie**

Both produce clean, consistent light. Godox brighter = faster shutter or lower ISO. Aputure bi-color = no gel needed for warm/cool adjustments.

### YouTube Talking Head
**Winner: Aputure 100x**

Bi-color means boleh match ambient light (window light changes throughout the day). Also quieter fan — microphone-friendly.

### TikTok/Reels
**Winner: Godox SL150III**

Brighter output = more flexibility with diffusion. Most Reels shoot dengan softbox besar anyway.

### Portrait Sessions
**Winner: Depends**

Godox if you have space and want maximum output. Aputure if you need quick color temp changes between clients.
