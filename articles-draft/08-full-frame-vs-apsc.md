Ini soalan paling common dekat YL Camera: "Boss, full frame ke APS-C?"

Jawapan salesman biasanya: "Full frame lah, better." Tapi truth is lebih complex. Dan banyak orang buat silap beli full frame, then regret sebab lensa mahal.

## Quick Technical Diff

| Aspect | Full Frame | APS-C |
|--------|-----------|-------|
| Sensor size | 36x24mm | ~23x15mm |
| Depth of field | Shallower (more blur) | Deeper |
| Low light | 1-2 stops better | Good, not great |
| Lens size | Bigger, heavier | Smaller, lighter |
| Lens price | 2-3x more expensive | Cheaper |
| Camera body | RM 6,000-25,000 | RM 2,500-10,000 |

## Use Case Decision Tree

### Instagram / TikTok Content → APS-C

You don't need full frame for social media. APS-C + good lens = identical results online. Plus lighter = more likely to bring everywhere.

**Best picks:** Sony A6400, Canon R50

### Wedding / Commercial Photography → Full Frame

Clients pay premium. They expect full frame. Low light churches, reception halls — full frame advantage real.

**Best picks:** Sony A7 IV, Canon R6 II

### Hobby / Travel → APS-C

Lighter, cheaper, less stress. Fujifilm X-T5 or Sony A6700 perfect.

### Hybrid Photo + Video → Depends

If video is 50%+ of income, consider APS-C with good video specs (A6700, R7). If photo is primary, full frame.

## Final Decision Framework

Ask yourself:

1. **Berapa budget TOTAL (body + lenses)?**
   - < RM 8,000 → APS-C
   - > RM 12,000 → Consider full frame

2. **What do I shoot most?**
   - Social media → APS-C
   - Client work → Full frame

3. **Do I mind carrying heavy gear?**
   - No → Full frame
   - Yes → APS-C

4. **Am I making money from this?**
   - No/hobby → APS-C
   - Yes, primary income → Full frame

## Bottom Line

**APS-C is not "worse." It's different.** Banyak professional photographer guna APS-C (especially Fujifilm). Don't let peer pressure push you into full frame if you can't afford the lenses.

**Panic mode advice:** Start APS-C. Make money. Upgrade to full frame when you have clients demanding it AND the lens budget.
