Soalan: Adakah ia worth it? Atau better beli Lowepro yang RM 400?

## Build Quality

Material: 400D nylon canvas shell, DWR coating. Zippers: weatherproof. Bottom: waterproof.

Ini bukan beg Shopee RM 80. Construction dia macam outdoor gear — think Arc'teryx or Patagonia level.

## Capacity Tests

**20L version:**
- Mirrorless body + 3 lenses
- 15" laptop
- Tripod (side carry)
- Small accessories

**30L version:**
- DSLR + 4-5 lenses
- 16" laptop
- drone (DJI Mini)
- More clothes/gear

FlexFold dividers adalah genius — boleh customize jadi shelves atau fold flat.

## Real Malaysian Scenarios

### LRT/Grab Commute
Slim profile means tak kena tolak orang. Quick-release straps easy to access. Tapi weight distribution — kalau pack heavy, shoulder straps rasa after 30 minit.

### Outdoor Shoots (Rain, Dust)
DWR coating works. Light rain — no problem. Heavy rain — better use rain cover (not included). Dust dekat construction site or beach — easy wipe off.

### AirAsia Carry-On Fit
20L fits under seat no problem. 30L fits overhead compartment. Both meet AirAsia carry-on dimensions.

**Worth it?**

**Yes if:**
- You commute daily with gear
- You travel often
- You value build quality over 5+ years
- You want beg yang "presentable" untuk client meetings

**No if:**
- You shoot sekali sebulan
- Budget tight (Lowepro 450 AW is 80% function at 50% price)
- You prefer roller bags

## Bottom Line

Peak Design V2 adalah investment. Kalau anda serious about photography as a career, this bag will outlast 3 cheap bags. Amortized cost = actually cheaper long term.

**Panic mode:** Beg tak buat duit. Camera buat duit. Prioritize gear first, bag later.
