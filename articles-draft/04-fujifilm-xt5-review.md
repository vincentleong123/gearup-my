Fujifilm X-T5. Kamera yang bila keluar, hampir semua Fujifilm fanboys dekat Malaysia terus pre-order. Tapi adakah ia worth the hype? Dan lebih penting — adakah ia sesuai untuk cara hidup dan shooting style orang Malaysia?

Saya bawa X-T5 untuk test ke Penang, Cameron Highlands, dan beberapa jalan dekat KL. Ini review jujur saya.

## First Impressions: Retro Cantik, Tapi Function Ke?

X-T5 ada dials dedicated untuk shutter speed, ISO, dan exposure compensation. Ini macam kamera film tahun 80-an. Untuk photographer yang suka tactile control, memang syurga. Tapi untuk beginner? Mungkin confusing.

Build quality solid. Weather-resistant. Dan berat dia just nice — tak macam Sony A7 IV yang rasa like brick.

## Film Simulations: Secret Sauce Fujifilm

Ini yang buat Fujifilm special. JPEG straight out camera yang looks like film.

| Simulation | Best For | Vibe |
|------------|----------|------|
| Classic Chrome | Street, documentary | Muted, editorial |
| Velvia | Landscapes, nature | Saturated, punchy |
| Acros | Black & white | Grainy, timeless |
| Eterna | Video | Cinematic, flat |
| Classic Neg | Everything trendy | Instagram aesthetic |

**Real talk:** Kalau anda malas edit (atau client nak deliver ASAP), shoot JPEG + Film Sim. Save hours of editing.

## Use Cases untuk Orang Malaysia

### 1. Balik Kampung Family Shoots
Raya, CNY, Deepavali. Family gathering dekat kampung. X-T5 dengan Classic Chrome atau Classic Neg hasilkan gambar yang sentimental dan timeless. 40MP means you can crop group photo jadi individual portraits.

### 2. Street Photography (KL, Penang, Melaka)
Petaling Street, Armenian Street, Jonker Walk. X-T5 discreet size + quiet mechanical shutter (yes, option for mechanical!) means you blend in. 7-stop IBIS = sharp shots pada shutter speed slow.

### 3. Travel Content (Langkawi, Sabah, Sarawak)
Lightweight untuk hike. Weather sealing untuk hutan hujan. Film simulations mean less editing time when you're exhausted in chalet.

### 4. Instagram-Worthy Content
Classic Neg hasilkan warna yang sedang trending dekat Instagram. Skin tones? Fujifilm is famous for pleasing skin tones, especially for Asian complexions.

## LCD Controversy: 3-Way Tilt vs Fully Articulating

Ini isu besar. X-T5 pakai 3-way tilting LCD (atas-bawah + tilt). Bukan flip-out like X-T4.

**Fujifilm's reasoning:** Photographers prefer this for waist-level shooting. Less bulky.

**Reality:** Vloggers dan solo YouTubers suffer. You cannot see yourself while recording.

**Verdict:** Kalau anda vlogger, X-T5 bukan untuk anda. Kalau pure photographer, actually 3-way tilt lebih useful daripada you'd think.

## Should You Buy It?

**Buy if:**
- You love the Fujifilm look
- You shoot stills more than video
- You travel often
- You appreciate physical dials
- You don't need vlogging LCD

**Don't buy if:**
- Video is your primary focus
- You're on a tight budget (lenses add up fast)
- You need the best autofocus (Sony still wins)
- You vlog alone

## Final Verdict

Fujifilm X-T5 adalah kamera yang "characterful." Bukan spec sheet warrior macam Sony. Tapi hasil gambar dia ada soul. Untuk photographer yang appreciate process of shooting AND final image quality, X-T5 adalah joy to use.
