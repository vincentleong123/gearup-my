## Real-World Tests: Malaysian Scenarios

### Test 1: Wedding Walk-Through
Lokasi: Dewan Gemilang, Shah Alam

RS 4 Pro dengan Sony A7 IV + 24-70mm f/2.8. Space sempit, banyak penjuru. Auto-tune motor response cepat. No jitter bila pusing corner. Native vertical shooting means boleh shoot TikTok/Reels content untuk client tanpa rebuild rig.

**Score:** 9/10

### Test 2: Outdoor Event (Humidity Test)
Lokasi: Taman Botani Putrajaya, 2pm, 85% humidity

After 3 hours continuous shooting, motors still smooth. No overheating warning. Tapi grip jadi sedikit slippery — recommend grip tape or gloves.

**Score:** 8/10

### Test 3: Run-and-Gun Corporate
Lokasi: Office tower, KL Sentral

Lift to lobby to meeting room. RS 4 Pro auto-lock bila power off means takde swinging camera accident. Sleep mode cepat. Balancing remains after power cycle (kalau tak tukar lens).

**Score:** 9/10

## Who Should Upgrade to RS 4 Pro?

### 1. Heavy Rig Shooters
Kalau anda pakai cinema cameras (FX6, C70, RED), RS 3 struggle. RS 4 Pro handle dengan confident.

### 2. Vertical Content Creators
Reels, TikTok, Shorts — native vertical shooting saves SO much time. No more L-bracket or rebuild.

### 3. Wedding Videographers with Big Budget
Dua tiga wedding job cover cost. And clients notice smooth footage.

### 4. RS 2 / Ronin-S Users
Kalau anda still pakai gimbal lama, this is a massive jump. Motor power, software, battery — semua improved.

## Who Should Keep RS 3?

### 1. A7 Series / Mirrorless Users
RS 3 handle A7 IV, R6, S5II dengan senang. No need extra torque unless you add heavy lens + monitor + mic.

### 2. Budget-Conscious Creators
RM 4,599 is a lot. RS 3 clearance at RM 2,500 is still excellent value.

### 3. Occasional Users
Kalau anda guna gimbal sekali sebulan, tak perlu latest.

## Malaysia-Specific Considerations

### Weather
Humidity is real. Always store gimbal dalam dry cabinet bila tak guna. Motor contacts can corrode over time kalau exposed to moisture constantly.

### Power
Malaysia uses 240V — charging is fine. But bawa extra batteries untuk wedding full day. Charging station dekat venue rare.

### Transport
RS 4 Pro masih muat dalam cabin bag AirAsia/MAS. Tapi combo case besar — might need check-in.

## Verdict: Upgrade or Not?

**Upgrade if:**
- You need 4.5kg payload
- You shoot vertical content regularly
- You're coming from RS 2 or older
- Income from videography > RM 5,000/month

**Stick with RS 3 if:**
- You shoot mirrorless only
- Budget is tight
- RS 3 still handles your rig fine
- You don't shoot vertical

**First gimbal?** Consider RS 3 clearance or RS 4 (non-Pro) unless you know you need the extra power.

## Final Thoughts

DJI RS 4 Pro adalah gimbal yang excellent — tapi "Pro" dalam nama tu memang meaningful. Ini untuk professionals yang push their gear. Bukan untuk casual users.
